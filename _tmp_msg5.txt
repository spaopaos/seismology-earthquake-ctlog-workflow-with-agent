Fix deformation maps: products are projected (UTM) GeoTIFF zips, not NetCDF GUNW

INSAR_GAMMA products deliver per-layer GeoTIFFs inside a zip: unw_phase,
corr, dem, inc_map (plus displacement rasters). 6_make_maps now extracts
the product zip, reads the layers with rasterio, warps the event
epicenter into the product CRS, crops a readable window around the
epicenter (--crop-km, default 40), plots axes in km-from-epicenter with
equal aspect, and writes the displacement GeoTIFF in the product native
CRS/transform (was wrongly hardcoded EPSG:4326). Validated on the four
Eryuan coseismic pairs: compact quadrupole lobes centered on the
epicenters at the mm-to-cm scale.
