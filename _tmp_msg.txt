- new skill seismic-insar: 4 launchers. 1_select_events clusters M>=threshold

  pair must bracket one threshold event alone (pairs spanning two events
  carry both signals; events inside one 12-day revisit are flagged
  inseparable). 2_run_downloader wraps `insarhub downloader -N S1_SLC
  --select-pairs`; 3_run_processor wraps `processor -N Hyp3_S1
  submit/refresh/download`; 4_collect_products builds the event-mapped
  manifest. Decision points (ml_min, buffer, window lengths) are
  user-confirmed; two config gates (insar.confirmed, insar.pairs_confirmed,
  mirroring the dbscan_eps_choice pattern) precede any scene search or HyP3
  submission; HyP3 free quota is spent only on user-confirmed coseismic pairs
