# Mindat LLM-Driven Workflow

A supervisor coordinates two data collectors and two plotters over OpenMindat. Structured arguments connect natural-language interpretation to actual mineralogical data queries and visualization functions.

The four action agents are Geomaterial Collector, Locality Collector, Network Plotter, and Heatmap Plotter ([zhang-2025-geoscience-workflow](../sources/zhang-2025-geoscience-workflow.md), PDF pp. 3–4).

The paper demonstrates networks and heatmaps. Broader task accuracy, recall, and pass-rate evaluation is proposed as future work (PDF p. 8). Treat demonstrations and aggregate benchmarks as distinct evidence.
