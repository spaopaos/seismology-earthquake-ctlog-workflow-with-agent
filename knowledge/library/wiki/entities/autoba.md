# AutoBA

AutoBA automates conventional multi-omics workflows from data descriptions and analysis goals. It plans steps, generates code, invokes external tools, and uses execution feedback for repair.

The paper evaluates 40 cases and distinguishes planning, setup/code generation, and end-to-end success. Adding Automated Code Repair raises reported end-to-end success from 26/40 to 35/40 under the evaluated configuration ([zhou-2024-autoba](../sources/zhou-2024-autoba.md), PDF p. 10).

Its relevance here is the design and evaluation of executable scientific workflows. The published bioinformatics results do not measure performance on seismic data.
