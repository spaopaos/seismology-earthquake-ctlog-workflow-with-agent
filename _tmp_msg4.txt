Add the swarm long-period InSAR product (second product mode)

Per user direction the insar stage now delivers TWO products:
1. per-event coseismic maps (existing, launchers 1-6)
2. swarm-scale long-period time series (new 7_swarm_timeseries.py) =
   InSARHub's standard chain end-to-end: merged swarm window -> quality
   network on selected stacks (integer PATH:FRAME tokens) -> whole-network
   HyP3 batch (bare submit auto-loads the stack's selected pairs) ->
   MintPy SBAS -> velocity.h5 / timeseries.h5 / cumulative deformation.
   The time series resolves inter-event transient/aseismic motion,
   complementing the per-event coseismic maps.

Skill documents both modes incl. quota reporting before swarm submit
(15-45 pairs/stack, ~10 credits each) and the SBAS-layer tropospheric
correction decision (MintPy correct_troposphere, per the cross-region
decision table).

Live on the Eryuan 3-month swarm: merged window 2025-01-23..2025-09-02,
stacks 33:502 (desc, 42 pairs) + 99:1265 (asc, 24 pairs) submitted to
HyP3 (~660 credits, 8% of the free monthly quota); a dedicated autopilot
polls, downloads and runs SBAS automatically.
